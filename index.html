KHR Production — Video Production Team Manager

KHR Production is a team app for video production: manage channels, scripts and tasks, assign work, track team performance and daily views, chat with your team, and create AI voiceovers.

  • Channels, scripts and task management
  • AI voiceover (Gemini) with multiple voices and styles
  • Team roles, approvals, performance and daily views
  • Works as a web app, Android app (APK) and Windows installer
